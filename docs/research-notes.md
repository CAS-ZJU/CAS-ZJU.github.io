# Research and content notes

Search and verification date: **2026-10-20**, Asia/Shanghai. This is a targeted author-based search (定向检索), not an exhaustive bibliography or a systematic review.

## Scope and selection

Identify work by the six members listed in `resources/person/persons.md` that directly concerns algorithms, AI, data labor, the social organization of computational knowledge, or critical assessment of computational social-science methods. English and Chinese works are eligible, with no fixed starting year; records after the search date are excluded. Journal articles, academic books, scholarly essays in collected volumes, and explicitly labeled preprints are admissible. Publications need not include all members.

The search is author-based rather than restricted to a journal ranking or index. No CSSCI/core status, impact factor, citation count, or affiliation was used as a proxy for article quality. The public page explains topical relevance; it does not claim to rate methodological quality. Full-text quality assessment was outside the request. Abstracts, accessible article text, author bibliographies, DOI metadata, and publisher descriptions were used to establish identity, bibliographic details, and topical fit. Even entries with accessible full text have **not** received a comprehensive methods audit.
