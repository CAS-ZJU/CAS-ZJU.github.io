## Tinkering Against Scaling

Our manifesto asks how academic researchers can understand and engage with algorithms as AI research becomes increasingly dependent on large models and costly infrastructure. We propose **tinkering**: hands-on engagement with smaller models and components as a practice of making, knowing, and caring.

Bolun Zhang, Yang Shen, Linzhuo Li, Yu Ji, Di Wu, Tongyu Wu, and Lianghao Dai.
